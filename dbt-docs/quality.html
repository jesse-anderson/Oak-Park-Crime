Geocoding

99.92% of incidents have coordinates (22,485 of 22,504). Only 19 do not. The pipeline is cache-first, so a run resolves almost everything without calling the geocoding API.

The chart below is not a cache-miss rate. dbt can only join the cache on lower(trim(location)), while the pipeline keys on normalize_location(), which also strips punctuation and standardises street suffixes. So this measures the dbt-side key, not the pipeline: 15,436 of the unmatched rows are geocoded perfectly well. It is here to quantify what publishing a real location_key upstream would buy, nothing more.

Zone assignment

Snapped rows fell within 55m of a polygon and were attached by the nearest-zone fallback. Outside-village rows are correct, not errors.
